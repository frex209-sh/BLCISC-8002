BLCISC-8002
Roblox "Build Logic!" CISC Processor. Model - 8002 (8-bit, second)
------
The instructions are in range from 1 byte to 3
3 Byte instruction: OPCODE / HIGH_BYTE / LOW_BYTE
2 Byte instruction: OPCODE / CONSTANT 
1 Byte instruction: OPCODE
------
Examples:
LD A, 2: 1000 00 00 / 0000 0010
MOV A, B: 1011 00 01
STR A, $1000: 1010 00 00 / 1000 0000 / 0000 0000
------
